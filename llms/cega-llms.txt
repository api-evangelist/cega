# Cega

> Cega is a decentralized exotic derivatives protocol building structured products (exotic options) for on-chain investors — Fixed Coupon Notes (pure options and bond + options) and Dual Currency Swaps — that generate yield with built-in downside protection. Live on Ethereum Mainnet and Arbitrum One (EVM) and on Solana. Backed by Pantera Capital.
